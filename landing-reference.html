Good to know

Clear, responsible and transparent.

Are the fitness plans medical advice?

No. General fitness and nutrition tools are educational and do not diagnose or treat medical conditions. Consult a qualified healthcare professional for medical conditions, injuries or therapeutic diets.

Can you build my gym's full app?

We start with a requirements discussion and a scoped quote. A responsive website or progressive web app is usually the lower-cost first step; native apps and complex integrations require separate planning and budget.

How does AI training work?

Workshops teach trainers how to use AI for drafts, structured planning, exercise alternatives and client communication. Trainers must verify all output and remain responsible for coaching decisions.

Can I pay monthly?

Cashfree Payments is the planned payment provider. Payment links, subscriptions and recurring AutoPay require account approval and technical integration; they are not active in this demo. Any recurring charge, renewal terms and cancellation process must be disclosed clearly before a customer subscribes.
